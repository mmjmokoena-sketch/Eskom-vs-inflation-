## Definitions to keep straight

- **CPI measure:** the CPI column is headline CPI (all items), annual average, from Stats SA table B2. CPIX (excluding mortgage interest) was the inflation-target measure in 2008 and averaged about 11.3% that year; do not mix the two.
- **Series break:** Stats SA covers primary urban areas up to December 2008 and all urban areas from January 2009, with compilation changes in January 2009.
- **Tariff measure:** the tariff column is the approved average increase for Eskom direct customers (from April). Municipal customers get a different increase from July (for example 8.61% against 9.61% in 2022/23).
- **Financial year:** year in the table is the calendar year in which the financial year starts, so 2008 means FY2008/09.
- **Average price (c/kWh):** definitions differ across compilations (for example 2008/09 shows about 24.7 c/kWh in one and 19.59 c/kWh in another), so this column is used only as a consistency check.
